# Campaign Brief: Glowmilk Spring 2026

- Client: Glowmilk Dairy Co-op
- Creator: Juno Acheampong
- Channel: short-form video and email newsletter
- Audience: parents of primary-school children in the Ostrey region
- Budget: GBP 18,500
- Flight: 2 March to 12 April 2026

## Objective

Lift trial of the new oat-and-milk lunchbox carton by 12 % in Ostrey stores.

## Key message

"Lunch they'll actually finish." Every asset ends on one clear CTA (call to
action): find the carton in the chilled aisle.

## Deliverables

- Six 15-second cut-downs, edited in Clipstack, master file
  `glowmilk_spring_cutdowns_v3.mp4`
- Two newsletter features, scheduled in Pinloom
- UGC (user-generated content) call-out asking parents to share lunchbox photos

## Measures

- CTR (click-through rate) on the newsletter link, target 3.5 %
- CPM (cost per thousand impressions) on video, ceiling GBP 6.40
- FQV score from the store panel, reported weekly

## Mandatories

- Co-op logo in the last two seconds of every video
- No claims about health benefits
