# Post-Campaign Review: Glowmilk Spring 2026

- Client: Glowmilk Dairy Co-op
- Creator: Juno Acheampong
- Reviewed by: Sabine Marlowe, Client Services Director
- Date: 28 April 2026

## Results

| Measure | Target | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carton trial uplift | 12 % | 9.4 % |
| Newsletter CTR | 3.5 % | 4.1 % |
| Video CPM | GBP 6.40 | GBP 5.85 |
| UGC posts | 150 | 212 |

The FQV score rose for the first four weeks and then fell back.

## What worked

- The newsletter outperformed: parents clicked the recipe link more than the
  store finder.
- UGC call-out produced 212 lunchbox photos; we used 14 in week 5.

## What to change next time

1. **One CTA per asset.** Two cut-downs had both a recipe CTA and a store CTA.
   Their completion rate was half that of the others. Keep one call to action.
2. **Brief the measures before the flight.** The store panel only started
   reporting in week 2. Agree every measure and its source in the brief.
3. **Name the master files.** Agencies asked for the wrong cut. State the
   master file name in the brief, as we did with
   `glowmilk_spring_cutdowns_v3.mp4`.
