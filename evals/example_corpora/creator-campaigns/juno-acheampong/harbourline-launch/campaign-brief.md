# Campaign Brief: Harbourline Night Crossing Launch

- Client: Harbourline Ferries
- Creator: Juno Acheampong
- Partner: Coastal Tourism Alliance (CTA)
- Channel: short-form video
- Audience: weekend travellers aged 25-40 in the Ostrey region
- Budget: GBP 24,000
- Flight: 18 May to 14 June 2026

## Objective

Sell 3,000 tickets for the new Friday night crossing in the first four weeks.

## Key message

"Leave Friday, wake up somewhere else." Each video ends with one CTA (call to
action): book the night crossing.

## Deliverables

- Eight 20-second videos, edited in Clipstack and posted through Pinloom
- One joint post with the CTA partner in week 3
- Launch results deck for the client after the flight,
  `harbourline-launch-deck.pptx`

## Measures

- CPM ceiling GBP 7.00
- CTR to the booking page, target 2.0 %
- Weekly reach, reported in the launch deck

## Mandatories

- Show the ferry at night in the first two seconds
- Partner logo only on the joint post

## Disclosure

Every creator post made under this brief is labelled "Paid partnership" in its
first line or in its first three seconds of video. Hollin & Pike Creative keeps
each signed creator agreement on file for two years after the flight ends.
