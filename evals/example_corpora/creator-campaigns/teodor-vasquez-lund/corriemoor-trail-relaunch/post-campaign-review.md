# Post-Campaign Review: Corriemoor Trail Relaunch

- Client: Corriemoor Outfitters
- Creator: Teodor Vasquez-Lund
- Reviewed by: Sabine Marlowe, Client Services Director
- Date: 16 June 2026

## Results

| Measure | Target | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trail 2 pairs sold | 4,600 | 4,870 |
| Podcast promo codes | 900 | 640 |
| Shops with display up in week 1 | 22 | 15 |

The FQV score held steady across the flight.

## What worked

- In-store display drove most of the sales; shops with the display up in week 1
  sold 31 % more pairs.
- The Sparkboard dashboard let shop managers see their own numbers.

## What to change next time

1. **Ship display kits a week early.** Seven shops put the display up late.
   Put the delivery date in the brief.
2. **Podcast codes undercount.** Listeners bought in store without the code.
   Brief a store-side measure for any audio channel.
3. **One CTA per asset.** The podcast spots used two calls to action (store
   and website). Keep one, as the brief said.
