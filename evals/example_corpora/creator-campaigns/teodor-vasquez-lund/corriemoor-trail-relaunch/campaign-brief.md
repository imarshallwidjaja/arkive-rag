# Campaign Brief: Corriemoor Trail Relaunch

- Client: Corriemoor Outfitters
- Creator: Teodor Vasquez-Lund
- Channel: podcast sponsorship and in-store display
- Audience: hillwalkers aged 35-60
- Budget: GBP 31,200
- Flight: 6 April to 31 May 2026

## Objective

Relaunch the Trail 2 boot and recover the 2025 sales level of 4,600 pairs over
the flight.

## Key message

"Same boot, new sole, no blisters." One CTA (call to action) everywhere: try
them on in store.

## Deliverables

- Four host-read podcast spots, 60 seconds each
- In-store display kit for 22 Corriemoor shops, artwork file
  `corriemoor_trail2_display_a1.pdf`
- Sparkboard dashboard for weekly sales by shop

## Measures

- Sales of Trail 2 per week, from Sparkboard
- Promo code redemptions from the podcast, target 900
- FQV score from the shop panel

## Mandatories

- Mention the 2-year sole guarantee in every spot
- No comparison with other brands

## Disclosure

Every creator post made under this brief is labelled "Paid partnership" in its
first line or in its first three seconds of video. Hollin & Pike Creative keeps
each signed creator agreement on file for two years after the flight ends.
