# Campaign Brief: Kettlecraft Winter Warmers

- Client: Kettlecraft Homeware
- Creator: Teodor Vasquez-Lund
- Channel: email newsletter and short-form video
- Audience: first-time home owners aged 25-35
- Budget: GBP 14,800
- Flight: 3 November to 19 December 2025

## Objective

Sell 2,400 Winter Warmer flask sets online before 19 December.

## Key message

"Hot at nine, hot at five." One CTA (call to action): order by 18 December
for delivery before the holidays.

## Deliverables

- Three newsletter features, scheduled in Pinloom
- Five 15-second videos, edited in Clipstack
- UGC (user-generated content) round-up of customers' winter walks

## Measures

- CTR on newsletter links, target 3.0 %
- CPM ceiling GBP 5.50
- Online orders by day, from Sparkboard

## Mandatories

- Show the 12-hour heat claim with its test reference KH-TR-118
- Delivery cut-off date in every asset

## Disclosure

Every creator post made under this brief is labelled "Paid partnership" in its
first line or in its first three seconds of video. Hollin & Pike Creative keeps
each signed creator agreement on file for two years after the flight ends.
