# Post-Campaign Review: Kettlecraft Winter Warmers

- Client: Kettlecraft Homeware
- Creator: Teodor Vasquez-Lund
- Reviewed by: Odile Brannagh, Strategy Lead
- Date: 9 January 2026

## Results

| Measure | Target | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flask sets sold online | 2,400 | 2,655 |
| Newsletter CTR | 3.0 % | 2.6 % |
| Video CPM | GBP 5.50 | GBP 4.95 |

## What worked

- Videos with the heat claim on screen had the lowest CPM.
- The UGC round-up was the most opened newsletter of the flight.

## What to change next time

1. **Subject lines.** Newsletter CTR missed target; two subject lines did not
   mention the delivery cut-off. Put the cut-off in every subject line.
2. **One CTA per asset.** Keep doing this; it worked.
3. **Report results against the brief's measures.** This review uses the
   brief's three measures in the same order. Keep that layout.
