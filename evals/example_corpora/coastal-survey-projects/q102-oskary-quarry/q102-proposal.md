# Proposal: Oskary Quarry Stockpile and Bench Volumes (Q102)

- Client: Oskary Aggregates Ltd
- Prepared by: Dmitri Halloway, Project Surveyor, Quillfen Geomatics
- Date: 9 March 2026
- Proposal reference: QG-PR-102

## 1. Purpose

Oskary Aggregates needs audited volumes of rock removed from the four working
benches of the Oskary pit during the 2025-2026 extraction year, for its royalty
return.

## 2. Scope of work

1. Fly the pit with the Lumora HX airborne laser scanner from a light aircraft.
2. Survey 18 ground control points (GCP) with RTK GNSS to check the laser data.
3. Register the new point cloud to the April 2025 baseline in SurfGrid.
4. Compute removed volume per bench and in total.
5. Deliver a technical memorandum and the volume table.

## 3. Deliverables

| Deliverable | File name | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Point cloud | `oskary_pit_2026.laz` | LAZ |
| Volume table | `oskary_pit_volume_2026.csv` | CSV |
| Technical memorandum | Q102 memo | PDF |

## 4. Programme

One flight day in late March 2026 and 6 working days of processing.

## 5. Assumptions

- The client provides the April 2025 baseline surface.
- Volumes are reported in cubic metres to the nearest 100 m³.

## 6. Fee

The fixed fee is GBP 21,700, excluding VAT.

## 7. Standard terms

Quillfen Geomatics carries out every survey under its Survey Quality Manual
QG-QM-04. Survey results describe the seabed or ground surface on the survey
dates only; they are not a design, dredging or volume certificate for any later
date.
