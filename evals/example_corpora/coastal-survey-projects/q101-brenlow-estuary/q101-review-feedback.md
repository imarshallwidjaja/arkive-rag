# Reviewer Feedback: Q101 Technical Memorandum

- Project: Q101 Brenlow Estuary Channel Survey
- Reviewer: Rufus Okonkwo-Lind, Principal Hydrographer
- Client: Brenlow Port Authority
- Date: 22 April 2026

## Overall

The memorandum is clear and short. The client can act on it. Please make the
changes below before issue.

## Comments

1. **Datum first.** State the vertical datum in the summary, not only in
   section 2. A reader who stops after the first paragraph must know the depths
   are below Chart Datum.
2. **Uncertainty at a stated confidence.** The TPU figure is correct, but every
   memorandum must give the confidence level next to the number, as done here.
   Keep this convention.
3. **Exact file names.** List every delivered file by its exact name with its
   contents. The deliverables table does this well.
4. **Coverage gaps.** The 70 % coverage area near the pontoon needs a figure or
   coordinates, so the client can see where the gap is.
5. **HWX tie-in.** Say when the tie-in was last repeated. The limitation is
   right, but put the date in the datum section as well.
6. **Change control.** The client has asked to extend the survey to the
   Ferrow marina. That needs a PCR (project change request) with its own fee
   before any field work.

## Decision

Approved for issue after comments 1, 4 and 5 are addressed.
