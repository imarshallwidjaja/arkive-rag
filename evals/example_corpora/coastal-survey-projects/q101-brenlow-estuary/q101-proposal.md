# Proposal: Brenlow Estuary Channel Survey (Q101)

- Client: Brenlow Port Authority
- Prepared by: Imani Castellane, Survey Lead, Quillfen Geomatics
- Date: 3 February 2026
- Proposal reference: QG-PR-101

## 1. Purpose

Brenlow Port Authority needs a current bathymetric surface of the inner estuary
channel before it schedules maintenance dredging. This proposal describes a
multibeam survey of 12.6 km² between the Saltgate breakwater and the Ferrow
road bridge.

## 2. Scope of work

1. Mobilise the survey launch *Kittiwake* with a Sonavex R4 multibeam echosounder.
2. Survey the channel at 100 % coverage with 20 % line overlap.
3. Position the launch with RTK (real-time kinematic) GNSS corrections from the
   Brenlow reference station.
4. Process soundings in Hydrolith 7 and grid them in SurfGrid at 50 cm.
5. Deliver a technical memorandum, a gridded surface and a sounding database.

## 3. Deliverables

| Deliverable | File name | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gridded surface | `brenlow_dtm_50cm.tif` | GeoTIFF |
| Survey lines | `brenlow_lines_2026.shp` | Shapefile |
| Technical memorandum | Q101 memo | PDF |

## 4. Programme

Field work takes 4 survey days in the neap tide window of 16-20 March 2026.
Processing and reporting take 9 working days after demobilisation.

## 5. Assumptions and exclusions

- Water levels are reduced to Chart Datum through the HWX tie-in at the
  Saltgate tide gauge.
- Intertidal areas above 0.5 m Chart Datum are excluded.
- Any change to the survey area is handled by a written change request before
  extra work starts.
