# Proposal: Fennick Canal Siltation Survey (Q103)

- Client: Fennick Waterways Trust
- Prepared by: Imani Castellane, Survey Lead, Quillfen Geomatics
- Date: 20 April 2026
- Proposal reference: QG-PR-103

## 1. Purpose

Fennick Waterways Trust wants to know how much silt has built up in the 7.8 km
reach between Hob Lock and the Carrow aqueduct since the 2021 survey.

## 2. Scope of work

1. Survey the reach from a small boat with a Sonavex R4 multibeam echosounder.
2. Position the boat with RTK GNSS; use a total station under the two road
   bridges where GNSS is blocked.
3. Process the data in Hydrolith 7 and compare it with the 2021 surface in
   SurfGrid.
4. Report silt thickness and volume per lock pound.
