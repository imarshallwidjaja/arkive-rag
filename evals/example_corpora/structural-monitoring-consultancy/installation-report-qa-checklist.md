# Installation report QA checklist (house template)

- Owner: Oluwaseun Brandt
- Revised: 10 January 2025

Use this list before any installation report is issued.

## Identity

- Report reference, client name and client contact are correct.
- Author, checker and approver are named.

## Method

- The correction method is stated: thermistor correction for vibrating-wire
  gauges, SFR temperature compensation for FBG arrays.
- Sensitivities and gauge factors come from certificates, not datasheets.
- The reference datum or baseline window is stated in full.

## Alarms

- Vibrating-wire gauges: amber 75%, red 100% of the designer's allowable change.
- FBG arrays: amber 60%, red 85% of the designer's allowable change.
- The TARP is referenced.

## Close

- The reliance clause names the client.
- The appendix schedule matches the installation drawings.
