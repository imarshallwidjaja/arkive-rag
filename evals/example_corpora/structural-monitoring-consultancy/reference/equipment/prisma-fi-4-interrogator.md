# Equipment sheet: Prisma FI-4 interrogator

- Maintained by: Maren Lisk
- Last reviewed: 14 July 2025

## Description

Four-channel FBG interrogator, 1 Hz to 100 Hz, with onboard storage of
one-minute means and a 4G router for upload to Marrick Atlas.

## Handling

Keep the interrogator in a ventilated cabinet between 0 and 40 °C. Clean every
connector before mating. Record the serial in the commissioning log and in the
installation report.
