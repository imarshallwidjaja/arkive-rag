# Regulatory summary: reservoir safety and the supervising engineer

- Prepared by: Callum Draycott
- Date: 4 March 2024

## Purpose

A crib sheet on how reservoir safety law affects our monitoring work for water
authorities.

## Categories

| Category | Consequence of failure | Supervising engineer visits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Loss of life likely | At least every six months |
| B | Loss of life possible | At least every twelve months |
| C | Limited consequence | At least every twelve months |

## What this means for us

- Our data may be read by the supervising engineer; state the reference datum
  and correction method in every report.
- For Category A reservoirs, provide telemetry and hourly readings.
- Do not change alarm levels without the supervising engineer's agreement.
