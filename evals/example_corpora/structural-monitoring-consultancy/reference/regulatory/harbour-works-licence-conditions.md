# Regulatory summary: harbour works licence conditions

- Prepared by: Hester Quayle
- Date: 27 August 2025

## Purpose

Crib sheet on the conditions harbour authorities attach to works licences that
cover our over-water monitoring.

## Usual conditions

- Levels stated to Chart Datum.
- Notice to the harbour office before diving, usually 24 hours.
- Marine mammal watch for piling or diving where the authority requires it.
- Navigation lights on any temporary structure left overnight.

## When the marine mammal watch applies

Only for piling or diving in the water. Land-side work on a quay or a substation
behind the sea wall does not need it.
