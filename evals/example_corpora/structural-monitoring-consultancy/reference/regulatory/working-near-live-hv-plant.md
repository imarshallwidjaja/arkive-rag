# Regulatory summary: working near live high-voltage plant

- Prepared by: Callum Draycott
- Date: 18 September 2024

## Purpose

Crib sheet for monitoring work inside electricity substations and cable
tunnels.

## Rules we follow

- Work only under the network operator's permit to work, issued by an
  authorised person.
- Keep the safety distances in the permit; never enter a fenced HV bay.
- Metallic cables near live plant need bonding and earthing agreed with the
  operator. Optical fibre is dielectric and needs neither.
- Cabinets inside the compound are fixed where the operator's engineer agrees.

## Records

Keep a copy of each permit with the commissioning log.
