## 1. Services

Marrick Instrumentation Ltd provides the services described in its proposal.
Anything not described there is extra and is charged at the current day rates.

## 2. Payment

Invoices are issued monthly and are payable within 30 days.

## 3. Liability

Our total liability is limited to the fee or GBP 1 million, whichever is
higher, and excludes indirect loss.

## 4. Reliance

Our reports are for the named client only. Third parties may rely on them only
with our written consent.

## 5. Instruments

Instruments remain our property unless bought by the client. Instruments left
in place at the end of monitoring are abandoned at the client's request.

## 6. Data

We keep monitoring data for six years after a job closes.
