# Proposal: Fibre Bragg grating strain monitoring of the Brackenford footbridge stay anchorages during re-tensioning

- Our ref: MI-PR-2611
- Client: Corrib Vale County Council
- For the attention of: Mr Aled Whitcombe
- Prepared by: Maren Lisk
- Date: 2 February 2026
- Validity: 90 days from the date above

## 1. Understanding of the brief

Corrib Vale County Council will re-tension the four back-stays of the
Brackenford footbridge, a single-pylon cable-stayed bridge over the River
Brack, after the 2025 inspection found the stay forces 8 to 12% below design.
Kelda Stressing Ltd will jack each stay in four steps. The council's designer,
Arden Lowe Associates, has set an allowable strain change of 160 µε at each
back-stay anchorage and wants live readings during every jacking step.

## 2. Proposed approach

- Install Lumenstrand LS-8 fibre Bragg grating arrays, each with a strain-free
  reference grating for temperature compensation.
- Log a 72-hour baseline after the adhesive cures and state the baseline
  window in the installation report.
- Set amber and red alarm levels at 60% and 85% of the designer's allowable
  strain change.
- Serve compensated strain on the Marrick Atlas portal with SMS and email
  alarms.
- Issue an installation and baseline report within two weeks of the baseline
  window closing.

### 2.1 Live monitoring during re-tensioning

One Lumenstrand LS-8 array would be bonded around each of the four back-stay
anchorage blocks, 32 gratings in all, read by one Prisma FI-4 interrogator in
a cabinet at the east abutment. During each closure weekend our engineer would
sit with the Kelda Stressing supervisor and show compensated strain refreshed
every ten seconds, so a jacking step can stop before the amber level is
reached.
