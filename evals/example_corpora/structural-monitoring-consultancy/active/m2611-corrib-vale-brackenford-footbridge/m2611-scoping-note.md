# Scoping note: Brackenford footbridge

- Job: M2611
- Client: Corrib Vale County Council
- Prepared by: Maren Lisk
- Date: 19 January 2026

## Background

Corrib Vale County Council contacted us about monitoring the Brackenford
footbridge stay anchorages during re-tensioning. The contractor needs alarm
levels in place before work starts.

## Site constraints

- Footbridge stays over the River Brack; anchorages reached from the deck.
- Bridge stays open to pedestrians outside the closure weekends.

## Options considered

| Option | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vibrating-wire gauges (Geovane VW-4200) | Robust, long record on our jobs, cheap to read | One cable per gauge; slower to install in numbers |
| FBG arrays (Lumenstrand LS-8) | Many gratings on one fibre, dielectric, fast sampling | Higher kit cost; needs an interrogator on site |

## Recommended scope

Fibre Bragg grating arrays at the four back-stay anchorages, read live during
re-tensioning.

## Open questions

- Will the stressing contractor share jack pressures live?
