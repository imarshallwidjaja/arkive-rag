# Scoping note: the Quarry Road embankment retaining wall

- Job: M2511
- Client: Corrib Vale County Council
- Prepared by: Priya Venn
- Date: 24 March 2025

## Background

Corrib Vale County Council contacted us about monitoring the Quarry Road
embankment retaining wall during widening. The asset owner wants to know
whether the works will strain the structure beyond what the designer allows.

## Site constraints

- Live carriageway alongside the wall.
- Wall face partly obscured by ivy.

## Options considered

| Option | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vibrating-wire gauges (Geovane VW-4200) | Robust, long record on our jobs, cheap to read | One cable per gauge; slower to install in numbers |
| FBG arrays (Lumenstrand LS-8) | Many gratings on one fibre, dielectric, fast sampling | Higher kit cost; needs an interrogator on site |

## Recommended scope

Twelve vibrating-wire gauges along the 60 m of wall nearest the widening.

## Open questions

- Can the ivy be cleared before installation?
- What allowable strain change will the designer set?
