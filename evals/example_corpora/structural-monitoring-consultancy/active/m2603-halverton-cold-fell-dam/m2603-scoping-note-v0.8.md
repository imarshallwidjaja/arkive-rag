# Scoping note: the Cold Fell Dam valve tower and outlet culvert

- Job: M2603
- Client: Halverton Water Authority
- Prepared by: Priya Venn
- Date: 5 January 2026
- Version: v0.8

## Background

Halverton Water Authority contacted us about monitoring the Cold Fell valve
tower and outlet culvert during refurbishment. The asset owner wants to know
whether the works will strain the structure beyond what the designer allows.

Cold Fell is a Category B reservoir, so the supervising engineer visits
yearly; we propose logger readings every 6 hours.

## Site constraints

- The outlet culvert is a confined space with one entry point.
- Winter access over the dam crest road only.

## Options considered

| Option | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vibrating-wire gauges (Geovane VW-4200) | Robust, long record on our jobs, cheap to read | One cable per gauge; slower to install in numbers |
| FBG arrays (Lumenstrand LS-8) | Many gratings on one fibre, dielectric, fast sampling | Higher kit cost; needs an interrogator on site |

## Recommended scope

Fourteen vibrating-wire gauges in the valve tower shaft and eight in the
outlet culvert.
