# Scoping note: Carrick footbridge

- Job: M2517
- Client: Northbray Rail Infrastructure
- Prepared by: Priya Venn
- Date: 19 May 2025

## Background

Northbray Rail Infrastructure contacted us about monitoring Carrick footbridge
during deck replacement. The contractor needs alarm levels in place before
work starts.

## Site constraints

- Footbridge spans four running lines; work only in possessions.
- Lift of the old deck planned for one weekend.

## Options considered

| Option | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vibrating-wire gauges (Geovane VW-4200) | Robust, long record on our jobs, cheap to read | One cable per gauge; slower to install in numbers |
| FBG arrays (Lumenstrand LS-8) | Many gratings on one fibre, dielectric, fast sampling | Higher kit cost; needs an interrogator on site |

## Recommended scope

Eight vibrating-wire gauges on the main girders, read every 10 minutes during
the lift weekend.

## Open questions

- Does the contractor want live readings in the lift cabin?
- Who owns the gauges after the job?
