# Scoping note: the East pontoon linkspan

- Job: M2617
- Client: Port of Kessick
- Prepared by: Priya Venn
- Date: 16 February 2026

## Background

Port of Kessick contacted us about monitoring the East pontoon linkspan during
hinge replacement. The asset owner wants to know whether the works will strain
the structure beyond what the designer allows.

## Site constraints

- Linkspan moves with the tide; gauges must tolerate cycling.
- Ferry timetable leaves four-hour windows.

## Options considered

| Option | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vibrating-wire gauges (Geovane VW-4200) | Robust, long record on our jobs, cheap to read | One cable per gauge; slower to install in numbers |
| FBG arrays (Lumenstrand LS-8) | Many gratings on one fibre, dielectric, fast sampling | Higher kit cost; needs an interrogator on site |

## Recommended scope

Six vibrating-wire gauges on the hinge girders.

## Open questions

- What hinge jacking load does the contractor expect?
