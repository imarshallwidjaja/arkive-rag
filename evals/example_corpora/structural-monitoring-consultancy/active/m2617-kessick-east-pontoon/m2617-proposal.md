# Proposal: Strain monitoring of the East pontoon linkspan during hinge replacement

- Our ref: MI-PR-2617
- Client: Port of Kessick
- For the attention of: Capt. Ewan Moss
- Prepared by: Priya Venn
- Date: 2 March 2026
- Validity: 90 days from the date above

## 1. Understanding of the brief

Port of Kessick has asked for strain monitoring of the East pontoon linkspan
during hinge replacement. The designer has set allowable strain changes for
the affected elements and wants live alarms against them.

## 2. Proposed approach

- Install Geovane VW-4200 vibrating-wire strain gauges with integral
  thermistors.
- Log on a Corlen CR-16 datalogger with hourly telemetry to the Marrick Atlas
  portal.
- Apply the thermistor correction to every reading and take initial readings
  as the reference datum.
- Set amber and red alarm levels at 75% and 100% of the designer's allowable
  strain change.
- Issue an installation report within two weeks of the initial readings.
