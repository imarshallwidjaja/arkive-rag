# Incident note IN-2508-01: Amber alarm from sheet piling vibration

- Job: M2508
- Client: Ostrand Energy Networks
- Date of incident: 9 July 2025
- Reported by: Tobias Achterberg

## What happened

Gauge BL-04 on the cable basement wall raised an amber alarm during vibratory
sheet piling 22 m away.

## Immediate action

The client contact was phoned within ten minutes and piling paused while
readings were reviewed.

## Cause

The vibratory hammer was driving through a band of gravel that transmitted
more vibration than expected.

## Effect on the data

BL-04 returned below amber within 40 minutes of piling stopping; no residual
change.

## Follow-up

- Contractor switched to a press-in rig for the nearest 12 piles.
- Alarm review letter sent to the client.
