# Incident note IN-2606-01: Thermistor fault on HE-11

- Job: M2606
- Client: Ostrand Energy Networks
- Date of incident: 23 February 2026
- Reported by: Tobias Achterberg

## What happened

Gauge HE-11 reported a thermistor reading of −40 °C, the logger's open-circuit
value.

## Immediate action

Readings from HE-11 were flagged on the portal and excluded from alarms.

## Cause

Water in the cable gland at the trench wall had corroded the thermistor
conductor.

## Effect on the data

HE-11 strain from 23 February is corrected with the temperature of HE-12, 1.2
m away.

## Follow-up

- Gland replaced and resealed.
- All trench wall glands checked on the next visit.
