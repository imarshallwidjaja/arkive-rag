# Scoping note: Old Quay Bridge

- Job: M2529
- Client: Corrib Vale County Council
- Prepared by: Priya Venn
- Date: 29 September 2025

## Background

Corrib Vale County Council contacted us about monitoring Old Quay Bridge
during parapet replacement. The contractor needs alarm levels in place before
work starts.

## Site constraints

- Tidal river below; access from the deck only.
- Parapet removal in short lengths under lane closures.

## Options considered

| Option | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vibrating-wire gauges (Geovane VW-4200) | Robust, long record on our jobs, cheap to read | One cable per gauge; slower to install in numbers |
| FBG arrays (Lumenstrand LS-8) | Many gratings on one fibre, dielectric, fast sampling | Higher kit cost; needs an interrogator on site |

## Recommended scope

Ten vibrating-wire gauges on the spandrel walls and arch ring.

## Open questions

- Is a marine licence needed for anything hung below the deck?
