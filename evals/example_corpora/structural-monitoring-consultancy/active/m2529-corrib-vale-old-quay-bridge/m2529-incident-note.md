# Incident note IN-2529-01: Near miss with a passing vehicle during a site visit

- Job: M2529
- Client: Corrib Vale County Council
- Date of incident: 12 November 2025
- Reported by: Tobias Achterberg

## What happened

A van entered the coned lane while our engineer was surveying gauge positions
on the parapet.

## Immediate action

Work stopped and the council's traffic management supervisor was told.

## Cause

A cone had been displaced by wind and not reset.

## Effect on the data

No effect on data; installation had not started.

## Follow-up

- Traffic management checks every hour added to the method statement.
