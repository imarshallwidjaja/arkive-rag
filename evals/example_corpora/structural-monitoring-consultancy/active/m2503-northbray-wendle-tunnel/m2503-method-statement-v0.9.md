# Method statement: strain gauge installation in Wendle Tunnel

- Job: M2503
- Client: Northbray Rail Infrastructure
- Author: Priya Venn
- Date: 29 January 2025
- Version: v0.9

## 1. Scope

Installation of 14 Geovane VW-4200 vibrating-wire strain gauges on the brick
lining of the north portal and the first 40 m of Wendle Tunnel, with a Corlen
CR-16 datalogger in the portal recess.

## 2. Sequence

1. Take the possession and set up protection at both ends of the work site.
2. Mark gauge positions from the drawing and photograph each brick face.
3. Rake out loose pointing and grind the bond area to sound brick.
4. Bond the mounting blocks with two-part epoxy and allow 12 hours cure before
   setting the gauge.
5. Set each gauge to mid-range, fit the cover and run the cable to the recess.
6. Connect the logger, read every channel and record the initial readings.

## 3. Hazards and controls

| Hazard | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Moving trains | Work only inside a possession with a protection controller |
| Dust from grinding | Extraction on the grinder; FFP3 masks |
| Working at height | Mobile tower inspected each shift |
| Epoxy skin contact | Nitrile gloves, eye protection; COSHH sheet on site |

## 4. Permits

Northbray Rail possession, permit to work for the portal recess and a hot
works permit for the grinder.

## 5. Emergency arrangements

The nearest A&E is Wendle General, 6 km by road. The protection controller
holds the emergency contact list.
