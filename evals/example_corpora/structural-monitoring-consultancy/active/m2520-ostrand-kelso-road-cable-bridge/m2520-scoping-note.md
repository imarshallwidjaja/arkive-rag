# Scoping note: the Kelso Road cable bridge

- Job: M2520
- Client: Ostrand Energy Networks
- Prepared by: Priya Venn
- Date: 2 June 2025

## Background

Ostrand Energy Networks contacted us about monitoring the Kelso Road cable
bridge during resurfacing. The asset owner wants to know whether the works
will strain the structure beyond what the designer allows.

## Site constraints

- Three 132 kV circuits cross inside the bridge.
- Road closures limited to Sunday mornings.

## Options considered

| Option | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vibrating-wire gauges (Geovane VW-4200) | Robust, long record on our jobs, cheap to read | One cable per gauge; slower to install in numbers |
| FBG arrays (Lumenstrand LS-8) | Many gratings on one fibre, dielectric, fast sampling | Higher kit cost; needs an interrogator on site |

## Recommended scope

Six vibrating-wire gauges on the cable trough supports.

## Open questions

- Can the gauges stay for the next resurfacing cycle?
