# Proposal: Fibre Bragg grating strain monitoring of the Greyscar Dam spillway

- Our ref: MI-PR-2505
- Client: Halverton Water Authority
- For the attention of: Ms Rowan Peake
- Prepared by: Maren Lisk
- Date: 7 April 2025
- Validity: 90 days from the date above

## 1. Understanding of the brief

Halverton Water Authority has asked for fibre Bragg grating strain monitoring
of the Greyscar Dam spillway. The asset owner wants a record of strain in the
affected elements before, during and after the works.

## 2. Proposed approach

- Install Lumenstrand LS-8 fibre Bragg grating arrays, each with a strain-free
  reference grating for temperature compensation.
- Log a 72-hour baseline after the adhesive cures and state the baseline
  window in the installation report.
- Set amber and red alarm levels at 60% and 85% of the designer's allowable
  strain change.
- Serve compensated strain on the Marrick Atlas portal with SMS and email
  alarms.
- Issue an installation and baseline report within two weeks of the baseline
  window closing.
