# Scoping note: the No. 4 crane rail beam

- Job: M2316
- Client: Port of Kessick
- Prepared by: Priya Venn
- Date: 30 October 2023

## Background

Port of Kessick contacted us about strain measurement during crane load
testing on the No. 4 crane rail beam. The contractor needs alarm levels in
place before work starts.

## Site constraints

- Test loads applied over one shift.
- Quay remains in operation either side.

## Options considered

| Option | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vibrating-wire gauges (Geovane VW-4200) | Robust, long record on our jobs, cheap to read | One cable per gauge; slower to install in numbers |
| FBG arrays (Lumenstrand LS-8) | Many gratings on one fibre, dielectric, fast sampling | Higher kit cost; needs an interrogator on site |

## Recommended scope

Eight vibrating-wire gauges on the rail beam at two sections, read every
minute during the test.
