# Incident note IN-2405-01: Gauge cable cut by contractor

- Job: M2405
- Client: Northbray Rail Infrastructure
- Date of incident: 14 June 2024
- Reported by: Tobias Achterberg

## What happened

The deck contractor's saw cut the cable of gauge TB-07 while trimming the
waterproofing membrane.

## Immediate action

The cable was spliced the same night and the gauge re-read.

## Cause

The cable route was not marked on the contractor's drawing.

## Effect on the data

TB-07 shows a 9 µε step at the splice; later readings are corrected for it.

## Follow-up

- Cable routes added to the contractor's drawing.
- Step correction noted in the next bulletin.
