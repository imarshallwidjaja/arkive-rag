# Letter: Holme Cutting monitoring complete

- From: Priya Venn, Marrick Instrumentation
- To: Mr Jarrow Keld, Northbray Rail Infrastructure
- Date: 14 September 2023
- Subject: End of monitoring, Holme Cutting retaining wall

The landowner finished regrading the crest on 1 September and the last two
weeks of readings show no further change.

The largest change over the job was 71 µε at HC-03, against an allowable 250
µε. No alarms were raised.

We propose to leave the gauges in place and recover the logger unless you want
monitoring to continue. Please let us know by 30 September.
