# Scoping note: Holme Cutting retaining wall

- Job: M2301
- Client: Northbray Rail Infrastructure
- Prepared by: Priya Venn
- Date: 3 February 2023

## Background

Northbray Rail Infrastructure contacted us about the crest regrading next to
the Holme Cutting wall. The asset owner wants to know whether the works will
strain the structure beyond what the designer allows.

## Site constraints

- Access only from the cess during possessions.
- The landowner's plant works above the wall crest.

## Options considered

| Option | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vibrating-wire gauges (Geovane VW-4200) | Robust, long record on our jobs, cheap to read | One cable per gauge; slower to install in numbers |
| FBG arrays (Lumenstrand LS-8) | Many gratings on one fibre, dielectric, fast sampling | Higher kit cost; needs an interrogator on site |

## Recommended scope

Eight vibrating-wire gauges on the pile caps of the four bays nearest the
regrading.

## Open questions

- Will the landowner share the regrading programme?
- Who receives out-of-hours alarms?
