# Installation of vibrating wire strain gauges: Holme Cutting retaining wall

- Ref: MI/2301/IR01
- Client: Northbray Rail Infrastructure
- Attention: Mr Jarrow Keld
- Prepared by: Priya Venn
- Checked: Callum Draycott
- Date: 21 March 2023

## 1. Introduction

Northbray Rail Infrastructure asked Marrick Instrumentation to install strain
gauges on the contiguous piled retaining wall between chainage 14.2 km and
14.6 km of Holme Cutting retaining wall before the regrading of the cutting
crest by the adjoining landowner. This note records the installation and the
initial readings.

## 2. Instrumentation

| Item | Type | Serial / batch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strain gauges | 8 No. Geovane VW-4200 vibrating wire, surface mounted | Batch GV-2301-36 |
| Datalogger | Corlen CR-16, 16 channel | CR16-0412 |
| Telemetry | 4G modem to Marrick Atlas | MOD-9604 |

## 3. Installation record

Gauges were installed from 6 to 8 March 2023. Mounting blocks were fixed with
two-part epoxy and each gauge was fitted with a steel protective cover. Cables
were run in flexible conduit to the logger enclosure.

| Gauge | Location | Orientation | Cable (m) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HC-01 | north face | horizontal | 24 |
| HC-02 | south face | horizontal | 21 |
| HC-03 | east face | longitudinal | 19 |
| HC-04 | west face | longitudinal | 21 |
| HC-05 | soffit | horizontal | 21 |
| HC-06 | top surface | horizontal | 14 |
| HC-07 | upstand | vertical | 45 |
| HC-08 | toe | horizontal | 16 |
