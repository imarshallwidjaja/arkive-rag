# Installation of vibrating wire strain gauges: Ashby culvert

- Ref: MI/2312/IR01
- Client: Corrib Vale County Council
- Attention: Mrs Nia Garroway
- Prepared by: Rafe Ilsley
- Checked: Priya Venn
- Date: 12 December 2023

## 1. Introduction

Corrib Vale County Council asked Marrick Instrumentation to install strain
gauges on the masonry arch barrel under the B4127 of Ashby culvert before the
abnormal-load movements over the culvert. This note records the installation
and the initial readings.

## 2. Instrumentation

| Item | Type | Serial / batch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strain gauges | 6 No. Geovane VW-4200 vibrating wire, surface mounted | Batch GV-2312-40 |
| Datalogger | Corlen CR-16, 16 channel | CR16-0388 |
| Telemetry | 4G modem to Marrick Atlas | MOD-1958 |

## 3. Installation record

Gauges were installed from 27 to 28 November 2023. Mounting blocks were fixed
with two-part epoxy and each gauge was fitted with a steel protective cover.
Cables were run in flexible conduit to the logger enclosure.

| Gauge | Location | Orientation | Cable (m) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AC-01 | crown | longitudinal | 31 |
| AC-02 | left haunch | vertical | 32 |
| AC-03 | right haunch | vertical | 35 |
| AC-04 | left springing | horizontal | 46 |
| AC-05 | right springing | longitudinal | 8 |
| AC-06 | spandrel | longitudinal | 32 |
