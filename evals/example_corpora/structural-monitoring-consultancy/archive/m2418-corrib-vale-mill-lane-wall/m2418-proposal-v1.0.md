# Proposal: Strain monitoring of the Mill Lane retaining wall

- Our ref: MI-PR-2418
- Client: Corrib Vale County Council
- For the attention of: Mrs Nia Garroway
- Prepared by: Priya Venn
- Date: 21 October 2024
- Version: v1.0
- Validity: 90 days from the date above

## 1. Understanding of the brief

Corrib Vale County Council has asked for strain monitoring of the Mill Lane
masonry retaining wall while a developer excavates for an underground car park
at its toe.

## 2. Proposed approach

- Install ten Geovane VW-4200 vibrating-wire strain gauges on the wall face at
  two levels.
- Log on a Corlen CR-16 datalogger with hourly telemetry to the Marrick Atlas
  portal.
- Apply the thermistor correction to every reading and take initial readings
  as the reference datum.
- Set amber and red alarm levels at 75% and 100% of the designer's allowable
  strain change.
