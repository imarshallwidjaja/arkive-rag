# Scoping note: Mill Lane retaining wall

- Job: M2418
- Client: Corrib Vale County Council
- Prepared by: Priya Venn
- Date: 23 September 2024

## Background

Corrib Vale County Council contacted us about monitoring the Mill Lane
retaining wall during a basement excavation at its toe. An earlier inspection
noted cracking that the client wants watched during the works.

## Site constraints

- Mill Lane is a bus route; work needs traffic management.
- The wall is listed; fixings need conservation consent.

## Options considered

| Option | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vibrating-wire gauges (Geovane VW-4200) | Robust, long record on our jobs, cheap to read | One cable per gauge; slower to install in numbers |
| FBG arrays (Lumenstrand LS-8) | Many gratings on one fibre, dielectric, fast sampling | Higher kit cost; needs an interrogator on site |

## Recommended scope

Ten vibrating-wire gauges at two levels on the wall face, fixed in mortar
joints to satisfy the conservation officer.

## Open questions

- Who provides traffic management?
- Will the developer pay for monitoring directly?
