# Scoping note: the Lockwood Reservoir draw-off tower, with an optional fibre-optic trial on the spillway

- Job: M2402
- Client: Halverton Water Authority
- Prepared by: Priya Venn
- Date: 15 January 2024

## Background

Halverton Water Authority contacted us about monitoring the Lockwood draw-off
tower during valve replacement. The contractor needs alarm levels in place
before work starts.

## Site constraints

- Tower reached by a 40 m access bridge with a 250 kg limit.
- Confined space entry into the dry well.

## Options considered

| Option | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vibrating-wire gauges (Geovane VW-4200) | Robust, long record on our jobs, cheap to read | One cable per gauge; slower to install in numbers |
| FBG arrays (Lumenstrand LS-8) | Many gratings on one fibre, dielectric, fast sampling | Higher kit cost; needs an interrogator on site |

## Recommended scope

Vibrating-wire gauges on the tower shaft at three levels, with an optional
fibre-optic trial on the spillway training wall.

## Open questions

- Does the authority want the fibre-optic trial priced separately?
- Is mains power available in the valve house?
