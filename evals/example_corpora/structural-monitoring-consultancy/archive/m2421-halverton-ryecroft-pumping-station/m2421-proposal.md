# Proposal: Strain monitoring of the Ryecroft pumping station wet well during pump replacement

- Our ref: MI-PR-2421
- Client: Halverton Water Authority
- For the attention of: Ms Rowan Peake
- Prepared by: Priya Venn
- Date: 11 November 2024
- Validity: 90 days from the date above

## 1. Understanding of the brief

Halverton Water Authority has asked for strain monitoring of the Ryecroft
pumping station wet well during pump replacement. The works are planned for
the coming season and the designer needs early warning of any strain change
beyond the allowable values.

## 2. Proposed approach

- Install Geovane VW-4200 vibrating-wire strain gauges with integral
  thermistors.
- Log on a Corlen CR-16 datalogger with hourly telemetry to the Marrick Atlas
  portal.
- Apply the thermistor correction to every reading and take initial readings
  as the reference datum.
- Set amber and red alarm levels at 75% and 100% of the designer's allowable
  strain change.
- Issue an installation report within two weeks of the initial readings.
