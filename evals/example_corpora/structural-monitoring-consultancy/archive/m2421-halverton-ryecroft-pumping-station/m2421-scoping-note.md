# Scoping note: the Ryecroft pumping station wet well

- Job: M2421
- Client: Halverton Water Authority
- Prepared by: Priya Venn
- Date: 21 October 2024

## Background

Halverton Water Authority contacted us about monitoring the Ryecroft wet well
during pump replacement. An earlier inspection noted cracking that the client
wants watched during the works.

## Site constraints

- Wet well is a confined space with gas monitoring.
- Station must keep one pump running.

## Options considered

| Option | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vibrating-wire gauges (Geovane VW-4200) | Robust, long record on our jobs, cheap to read | One cable per gauge; slower to install in numbers |
| FBG arrays (Lumenstrand LS-8) | Many gratings on one fibre, dielectric, fast sampling | Higher kit cost; needs an interrogator on site |

## Recommended scope

Six vibrating-wire gauges on the wet well walls at the pump mounting level.

## Open questions

- Is a gas-safe logger enclosure needed?
