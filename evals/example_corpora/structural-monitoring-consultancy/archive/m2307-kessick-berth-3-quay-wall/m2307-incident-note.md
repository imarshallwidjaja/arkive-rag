# Incident note IN-2307-01: Logger flooded on a spring tide

- Job: M2307
- Client: Port of Kessick
- Date of incident: 21 September 2023
- Reported by: Tobias Achterberg

## What happened

A spring tide with an onshore wind overtopped the quay and filled the logger
enclosure at bay 13 with sea water.

## Immediate action

The logger was switched off, recovered and replaced with a spare the next
morning.

## Cause

The enclosure was mounted 0.3 m lower than the agreed level to clear a
bollard.

## Effect on the data

Readings from 02:00 to 09:40 are missing. The gauges themselves were not
affected.

## Follow-up

- Enclosure raised to the agreed level.
- Spare logger kept at the port office during spring tides.
