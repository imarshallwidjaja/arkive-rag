notes for anyone picking through this folder

this is the Marrick Instrumentation job archive. archive/ has jobs closed by the
end of 2024, active/ has the 2025-26 jobs, reference/ has the equipment sheets
and the regulatory crib sheets. rate cards sit at the top level, one per year.

job folders are named by job number then client then site, so m2409 is the
Askern viaduct job for Northbray Rail. some reports were drafted in versions
(v0.8, v0.9, v1.0); only v1.0 was issued, the drafts are kept for the QA trail.

most of our strain jobs are vibrating-wire gauges. the fibre Bragg grating jobs
are the newer ones and have their own reporting rules, so don't mix the two up.

the Outer Jetty report has an over-water section the harbour master insisted on.

a couple of the calibration sheets got copied into more than one job folder when
the same interrogator went to two sites. they're the same file.
